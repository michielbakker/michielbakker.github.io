Hi! I'm an Assistant Professor at MIT with appointments in the Institute for Data, Systems, and Society (IDSS), the Laboratory for Information and Decision Systems (LIDS), the Sloan School of Management, and the Center for Constructive Communication (CCC) in the MIT Media Lab. My research focuses on large language models (LLMs), AI safety, AI governance, and the societal impacts of AI. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Google DeepMind, where I worked on post-training and safety for the Gemini models.

Before that, I was a Computer Science PhD student at MIT, working with Alex "Sandy" Pentland at the MIT Media Lab. I also hold a bachelor's and master's degree in physics from TU Delft, where I worked on quantum computing at QuTech and IBM Quantum.
