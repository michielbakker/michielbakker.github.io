Policy

I care a lot about making the transition to a post-AGI world go well, and I think policy work is a big part of that. I co-led the Nationaal AI Deltaplan, written at the request of the Dutch Ministry of Economic Affairs, and I co-wrote Europe 2031 (audio version), a scenario about what getting AI wrong would mean for Europe. It has been read by over a million people and was covered by the Economist, the Guardian, Bloomberg, and the New York Times, as well as many national media outlets across Europe.
